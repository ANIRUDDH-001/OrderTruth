# Phase 6 competition scorecard

**Reviewer judgment / HYPOTHESIS, not official scores or winning odds.**

| Equally weighted criterion | Provisional /10 | Demonstrated merit / limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| Technological Implementation | 6 | Durable recovery and adversarial tests; current live AI and genuine webhook path unqualified. |
| Design | 7 | Coherent responsive case→approval→receipt; full accessibility and independent user assessment absent. |
| Potential Impact | 5 | Specific reference-merchant problem; integrations, buyers and operational savings unproven. |
| Innovation / Idea | 5 | Plausible focused evidence-linked recovery; reconciliation/idempotency/investigation have existing alternatives. |
| Presentation | 2 | Actual journey is demonstrable; final video/submission not reviewed or produced. |

Five skeptical perspectives: PayPal engineer demands provider boundaries; AI engineer demands incremental value/safety; finance operator demands correction scope and reusable proof; designer demands guest entry/recovery states; founder demands integration cost and a validated buyer. Strongest distinction: independently verifiable approved merchant correction. Biggest reason to lose: weak/unavailable AI behind a technically elaborate story.
